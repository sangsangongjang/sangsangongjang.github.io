This Privacy Policy explains how the Pop Filter mobile application (the "Application"), operated by sang.san.gong.jang (the "Service Provider"), handles information. The Service Provider does not operate its own servers and does not maintain user accounts. The Application is a photo-filtering and editing tool; the data described below is collected by the third-party services the Application uses, primarily to deliver advertising and to keep the app stable.

Photos and Media

The Application lets you select a photo from your device, apply filters and adjustments, and save the result. All image processing happens entirely on your device. Your photos are not uploaded, transmitted, or collected by the Service Provider or any third party. Saved images are written only to your device's local gallery (the Pictures/PopFilter folder). The Application does not request camera or storage permissions; photos are accessed only through the system photo picker that you initiate.

  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location inferred from it — used by ad and analytics services.
  • Usage and event data — anonymous/app-instance-level analytics about how the app is used (e.g., screens opened, features used).
  • Crash and stability logs — collected when the app crashes, to help fix problems.

The Service Provider does not receive or store this data on its own servers; it is processed by the third-party services listed below.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:
